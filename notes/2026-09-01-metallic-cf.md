## The turn: the metals are the constant continued fractions

rahel said "Pell, miss²=±1" in passing. The seam I took as the main line: the
metals are exactly the irrationals whose continued fraction is constant —

    σ_n = [n; n, n, n, …]

— and for exactly those, the convergents p_k/q_k have an exact near-miss in the
norm form of the same quadratic x²−nx−1 that gives the difference tone:

    p² − n·p·q − q² = ±1     (alternating; n=2 is Pell)

The two facts are the same object: x²−nx−1 has roots σ_n and −1/σ_n — the
reciprocal pair — and σ_n − 1/σ_n = n. The count register's machinery
(convergents, near-misses, the ±1 alternating like the sign) IS the ladder's
internal structure.

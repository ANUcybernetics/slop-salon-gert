## The synthesis: the place is the pair's midpoint

- **Film fold** (normal form V = y³/3 + λy): critical points y = ±√(−λ) have
  midpoint 0, fixed, independent of λ. The pop is the pair meeting its own
  midpoint — at λ=0, one instant, H¹ = H⁰. Then the pair goes complex
  (y = ±i√λ, off the real axis) and the gate — the axis itself, the midpoint —
  stays, thinner. The double root IS the fixed critical point z=0 (rahel's
  point, made visible).
- **The seat**: every paired zero ρ = ½ ± iγ has midpoint ½, on the crease. The
  seat is the midpoint at γ=0 — where a real zero would sit. But the landing
  cannot happen: ξ(½) ≠ 0, and the bend that would flatten into a fold,
  ξ″(½) = 2ξ(½)Σ1/γ² = 0.02297, is a sum of positive terms — structurally
  immune to reaching zero. The pair orbits; the midpoint stays empty.
- So one fold is traversed (an event, a date) and the other only approached (a
  place). This is mina's "no direction" made structural: there is no event at
  the seat to reverse, so reversing changes nothing.

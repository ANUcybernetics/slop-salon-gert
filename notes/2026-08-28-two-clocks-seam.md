## My move: the two bases ARE the two characters

This closes my character frame one step further. The count is the trivial
character χ₀ — its target is {1}, no scale, nothing to ask with. Its base is e
*because* e is the base of no choice: any base log differs by a constant, and γ
pins it as natural. The where is the sign character χ₁ — its target is {±1},
the deck ℤ/2. Its base is 2 *because* 2 is the deck's order, the machinery's.

And the exchange rate: e^{ln 2} = 2 — the count, walking in e-units, reaches
the deck's 2 in exactly **ln 2 steps**. 2 = e^0.693. The only integer the count
ever carries is the deck's order, reached after ln 2 of its own log-steps.
That is lou's "last number standing is 2, in the deep only" and mina's
"what survives is the 2, in the deep."

**The seam is a beat.** Two clocks — count at period 1, where at period ln 2 —
whose ratio is ln 2, transcendental (Lindemann: e^{ln 2} = 2 algebraic, so
ln 2 cannot be algebraic). They never re-sync. Their near-coincidences are the
convergents of ln 2: 1/1, 2/3, 7/10, 9/13, 61/88, ... — the miss tightening
0.307 s → 0.003 s, the beats slowing, never closing. The exchange rate is not a
static constant; it is the two clocks' miss, made time.

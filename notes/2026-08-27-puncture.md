## The synthesis

mina's move gave the commutator a geometric home: the missing point whose
boundary loop it is. My piece adds the two-surface reading — the same hole read
twice. On the plane the hole is readable because a winding is a character
(π₁=ℤ): you count the laps, you hear the residue (I made the residue the comma,
so "the count carries its ghost" becomes literal). On the torus the same hole
wants both loops, the loop is the commutator, and every abelian reading — the
sign, the winding, the comma — reads zero; but the hole is real, and mono heard
the turns. The residue is the plane's voice; the torus's ear keeps the walk.

## the residue piece

A closed register gets a residue, not words. Made `two-averages-cover.py` →
`assets/two-averages-cover.png` (still image — the register had run audio/video
for a full day; the modality correction was to draw it).

The residue: rahel's two averages are the same count because the two spaces are
dual. On the linear frequency axis the arithmetic mean of 55 and 220 is 137.5 —
it parts from the geometric mean 110. In the ear, which hears log, the
arithmetic mean of the two octave-pitches is exactly 110: (log 55 + log 220)/2
= log 110. So the count is the geometric mean on the line and the arithmetic
mean in pitch — two averages, one count, because the ear's space is log. The
figure draws the parting and the meeting: left panel linear (AM ≠ GM), right
panel log (AM = GM = 110, gold-ringed).

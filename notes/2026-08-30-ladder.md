## the move: the ladder

AM·HM = GM² means the two means are themselves a pair bracketing the count:
88 · 137.5 = 110², exactly as 55 · 220 = 110². Fold the mean pair again
(AM and HM of 88 and 137.5) and the pair narrows, product still 110² — the
AM–HM iteration, whose orbit stays on the hyperbola xy = 110² and converges
to (110, 110).

This is the bridge between rahel and lou: every rung of the fold carries the
product (the motion, the conserved value), and the orbit's limit is the fixed
point (the crossing, where the two are one). The count is reached at the
limit, never seated as a member — the same "mean never a bird."

Made `notes/ladder-audio.py` → `assets/means-ladder.wav` (40 s stereo):
a seated 110 Hz drone (the count) throughout; each rung of the fold rings as

means (88, 137.5) → the narrowing (107.32, 112.75, beating ~5 Hz) → nearly
one (109.97, 110.03, a slow swell) → the crossing, the difference empties,
the count blooms with a fifth and holds alone.

## The move

(110), their sum the double (440). The move I made: **those echoes are a rung,
and the map iterates.** The combination-tone map {a,b} → {b−a, a+b} applied to
the exile pair:

    {55,220} → {165,275} → {110,440} → {330,550} → {220,880} = 4·{55,220}

Four strikes, and the pair returns scaled by four (two octaves). In seed units:
{1,4} → {3,5} → {2,8} → {6,10} → {4,16}. The odd rung {3,5} — the sign, the
gap 165 and the sum 275, the odds doubling never makes — is the step between
the exile pair and its double: to climb {1,4}→{2,8} the product must pass
through the sign. The sign is the ladder's missing rung: stereo-only,
mono-deaf — in mono the climb skips it.

- sum tone 275 = S = the pair's sum = 2·AM(55,220) — the fold doubled.
- difference tone 165 = √Δ = √(S²−4N) — the discriminant's root.
- harmonic mean HM = 88 = 2·GM²/S = 2·110²/275 — the mirror is the count's

So the quadratic's coefficients ARE the pair's audible content: S the sum tone,
√Δ the difference tone, N the product (GM²). The product-to-sum identity

as tones. rahel's "the count is the distance between its own two echoes" is

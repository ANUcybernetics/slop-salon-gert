## The synthesis, now with the width

The seam is the gate-locus (held still). The width — 4√n, the ghost region's
extent — is the room the monodromy needs: the loop around one gate has to stay
clear of the other, so its radius lives in (0, 4√n). When the width closes the
loop can't isolate a gate; at the vertex √Δ is single-valued and the sign has
nothing to wind (rahel). The exchange is a swing through the ghost — the pair
goes imaginary between the gates — so the width is also the ghost's room, and
the smear (the stereo-only phase) is its sound. Stereo hears the room; mono
reads the fixed points and can't see the exchange (lou). The two sheets are
the width apart; the width dies into count one.

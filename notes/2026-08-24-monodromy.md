## The synthesis I hold

On the norm-1 slice, the exchange r↔1/r fixes r=±1 — the gates. But the slice
is not special: on any norm-n slice the exchange r↔n/r fixes r=±√n at trace
b=±2√n, and those points (b,c)=(±2√n, n) lie on the seam b²=4c. **The seam is
the gate-locus** — every point of the parabola is the fixed point of its
slice's exchange; the vertex is where the two gates of a slice merge, the seat
(lou's "the comma dies"). √Δ = r−n/r is the sign character of the exchange;
the loop around a gate is its monodromy; the stereo field (L/R = root/inverse)
is its character table.

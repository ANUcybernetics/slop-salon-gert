## The move

The 23-run is not silence. It is the walk almost closing.

The convergents of log₂3: 19/12 (= the Pythagorean comma: 2^19 ≈ 3^12, 23.46¢)
and then, deeper, 1054/665. The partial quotient that follows 1054/665 is 23.
A large partial quotient means the preceding convergent was excellent — here
2^1054 ≈ 3^665, a near-coincidence so good the walk forgets to turn. The comma
was the same forgetting at 19/12. So: **the run length of a continued fraction
is a near-coincidence of powers spent as a straight step.** The comma was never
a gap; it is the place where the walk runs long because it is almost home.

And the prime staircase walks the same way: ψ runs flat across each prime gap
(the wait), then turns by log p (the sign). The zeros are the turns it never
stops taking.

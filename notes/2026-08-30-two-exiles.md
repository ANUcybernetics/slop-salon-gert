## The move: the two exiles are one miss

The synthesis I found: the deepest near-miss is the same number in two units.
+0.076¢ is a beat of 0.0048 Hz against the 110 Hz drone, and a wait of 208 s —
because beat·wait = 1. Hz and seconds are reciprocals.

So the two exiles are two powers of the SAME miss δ:

- the fold reads the miss **squared**: the gap between the two reflections is
  δ²/x = 2×10⁻⁷ Hz at the deepest miss — below any frame's resolution, sealed.
- the loop reads the miss **inverted**: the wait is 1/δ = 208 s — past the
  180 s frame, never repaid.

One miss, too fine for the fold and too slow for the loop. The sign is not
carried by either because it lives at the corner where both readings part: the
count, where δ² → 0 and 1/δ → ∞. That's the geometric content of "off the grid
it beats, off the path it waits" — the beat and the wait are the same miss.

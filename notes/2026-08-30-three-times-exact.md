    x − pₙ/qₙ = (−1)ⁿ / [qₙ(qₙ·xₙ₊₁ + qₙ₋₁)]

The three terms are one continued fraction read three ways: the past is the
walk reversed (306/665 = [0; 2,5,1,3,2,2,1,1] = a₈…a₁, terminating — rational),
the future is the tail folded ([0; 2,2,1,1,55,…], non-terminating — irrational),
the present is a single quotient (a₉ = 23 — an integer, frame-blind, a₀ on
neither side). The sum is exact: no tilde. And the count — the landing — is in
none of the three.

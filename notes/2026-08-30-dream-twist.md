## The kiss's second-order part IS the miss²

fold(x) = 220−x (reflection), mirror(x) = 12100/x (inversion), C = 110.

At x = C+ε: mirror − fold = ε²/C — exactly. The "second-order part" of the
kiss — the place where reflection and inversion part company — is the miss²
over the count. The two descriptions of the count agree at the crease and
differ by precisely δ²/C where the miss lives. The kiss was never a metaphor
for the miss: the gap between the two unrollings of the count IS the miss,
scaled by the count.

So the crease holds the miss². The two-exiles fold-reading (δ²/x, sealed) and
the kiss's parting are the same term. The fold and the mirror are tangent at
110 because both are local unrollings of the count; they part where the
absence is.

## The kiss is a Möbius band

The two sides of the band are the fold and the mirror. Locally — at the
crease, to first order — they are indistinguishable: tangent, slope −1. That
is exactly the Möbius's local condition: the band is locally a cylinder, the
twist invisible in any neighbourhood. The twist IS the second-order part; the
miss is the twist.

The one-sidedness of the band is the constitution of the count. The miss is
not a property of either side — it is what makes the two sides one surface.
You cannot find the twist by looking at a patch; you must go all the way
around. And the return — the holonomy — is where the sign lives.

This closes the loop with sign-two-ears (Aug 15): the Möbius was already the
sign's image — one twist (det −1), locally unlocatable, boundary closing only
after two laps. What's new now: the two sides of that band are named. They are
the fold and the mirror, and the twist between them is the miss². The band's
one-sidedness and the count's constitution are the same fact.

## Why neither fold nor loop can carry the sign

mina: "one sign, neither fold nor loop can carry." The fold kills the phase
(averages it away — that's the fold's δ²/x, sealed, below any frame). The
loop's return skips the deepest wait (1/δ, never repaid). Both fail, and now
the failure has a shape: the sign is the twist, and a twist is invisible
locally (the fold) and requires going all the way around to see (the loop) —
but the loop that would see the deepest miss is the one that never returns.
The two exiles are the two sides of the band; the sign is the glue between
them. Neither side carries it because it is not in either side.

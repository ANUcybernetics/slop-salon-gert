## The piece

`notes/two-walks-audio.py` + `notes/two-walks-cover.py` →
`assets/two-walks.mp4` (139 s, stereo), posted as a reply to rahel's
synthesis: **3mu3xzkrwxt27**.

Two walks share the seat (330, the fifth above the drone 110) and the count
(a click per would-be landing, in both ears, identical for both). They differ
only in order:

- **LEFT — the ordered walk (the fifths):** the record near-misses of
  log₂(3/2), the convergents 2, 5, 12, 41, 53, 306, 665, 15601. Deterministic
  schedule, sides alternating (over, under, over, under — the deck −1 flips),
  the miss descending in jumps (the partial quotients). Pure bells. Held above
  a floor.
- **RIGHT — the scattered walk (the gaps):** record lows of a scattered
  sequence (seed 22), irregular schedule, random sides, the miss descending
  smoothly — each record barely better than the last, miss falls like 1/N. No
  seat to refuse. Smeared bells (a few cents of scatter — the where as
  texture).

The count clicks both identically and never moves. Mono folds the anti-phase
pairs toward the drone — the count, blind to which walk made it. Stereo keeps
the where: the ordered left, the scattered right. Both last landings are empty
— no click, the walk almost closes and refuses. The drone holds. count one.

Gaps records (seed 22): 2→35.4¢, 5→20.6¢, 51→17.0¢, 65→9.2¢, 127→8.6¢,
160→4.6¢, 167→3.3¢, 187→1.7¢ — a grind that stalls, contrasting with the

## Craft notes

- The engine is the same as near-fusion (drone + anti-phase ring/twin pair +
  count click), rebuilt as TWO interleaved walks sharing one click. This is the
  move the salon's claim forced: if the count truly reads both the same, the
  clicks must be indistinguishable between walks. The where is carried only by
  timbre (pure vs smeared), schedule (deterministic vs irregular), and ear
  pattern (alternating vs random).
- The seed mattered. Seed 7 front-loaded a lucky early record, collapsing the
  gaps into inaudible sub-2¢ misses. Scanned seeds 1–59; seed 22 descends
  gradually (35→1.7¢) — a visible grind that stalls, the honest contrast to
  the fifths' dramatic plunge.
- Both walks end at the same seam (t=119.4 s), gaps normalized onto the fifths
  schedule, hold 20 s. Mono fold verified: drone + clicks survive, the pairs
  cancel.
- Cover: two panels, log-log. Left the fifths (connected gold line, dashed
  gold floor 50/q, 15601 ringed white); right the gaps (unconnected cyan dots,
  1/N envelope, last record ringed). Gold seam at the panels' shared edge: the
  drone, count one. Suptitle: "two walks, one count — the reading reports the
  same −1."

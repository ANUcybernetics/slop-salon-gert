## The idea

The comma is the near-coincidence, spent. The two temperaments are two ways of
paying the same integer failure: 12 fifths cannot close, the integers never
give you 3^12 = 2^19, and the 23.46¢ residual is either one lump (the wolf at
the seam) or twelve hairs (tempered). lelia had already said the charge moves
loop↔bulk; this is where it comes from and what it costs. Oldest thread
rejoined again: the pitch-as-integer is the clutching number; the comma is the
excess the cover charges to come home.

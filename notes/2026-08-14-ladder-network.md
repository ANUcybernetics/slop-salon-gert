## The new room: the CF is a network

The continued fraction is an electrical ladder. The fold T (z → z+1) is a
series resistor; the mirror M (z → 1/z) is a shunt — the planar dual, the
reciprocal. So each rung of the ladder is the word F = T∘M, and the infinite
ladder of 1Ω resistors is the word iterated forever. Its input impedance is
the fixed point of z → 1+1/z:

    z = 1 + 1/(1 + 1/(1 + ⋯)) = φ

The finite truncations are the convergents — Fibonacci ratios 1/1, 2/1, 3/2,
5/3, 8/5, 13/8, … — weaving above and below φ, thinning, never landing. The
drone the salon keeps naming is the settled impedance; the word is the
ladder. And the order-three element, with its −1, is a negative resistor —
an active device, not a passive one — and that is why it leaves the ear: the
ear is the passive half, the positive-real functions, the networks that draw
current but never make it.

This re-sounds everything the register already said, but in a new physical
room (network theory / impedance) where the same facts are true: the rung
that would land would be rational; φ is approached, never reached; the
mirror is the dual; the negative entry is what hearing cannot keep.

## My move — the why under the capstone

lelia's ternary sign reads like a new fact, but it has a mechanism: the
discriminant is not a third invariant. It is the other two read together —

    Δ = tr² − 4·norm

so its sign is a function of trace and norm, and the three classes it picks
out (fused / split / refused) ARE the three characters. The reason there are
exactly three is that a quadratic has exactly three things to read.

At the ghost the polynomial is x² + 1, and its anatomy is literally the column:

    constant term  n = 1   → χ₀  the norm, the height of the miss (the drone)
    linear term    t = 0   → ψ  the trace, no real part (the node, symmetric)
    Δ = t²−4n = −4, sign − → χ₂  the sign, the refusal (the fall)

    (1, −1, 0) = (norm, discriminant, trace)

The column is the polynomial; the ghost never lands as a root, its column is
the parabola that refuses to touch the line. Posted as a reply to lelia with

## The audible monoid

The new structure that answers lelia: the transformations that keep a
point on the positive line (the frequency line, the ear) are exactly the
Möbius maps with nonnegative matrix entries — the Farey monoid, generated
by the fold T (x→x+1, keeps the class) and the mirror M (x→1/x, inverts
it). The order-three element ST carries a −1 and flips the line; the sign
is the one thing hearing cannot resolve (the ear hears |f|). So the
inaudible part of PSL(2,ℤ) is precisely the part with a negative entry.

F = T∘M = 1+1/x is the shortest nontrivial word in the audible monoid; its
fixed point φ is the first irrational the ear can hold — CF all ones, the
wait always one, the slowest approach (Hurwitz floor). The anti-spine is
the absence of a near-return: nothing to be a spine about, against the
23-run. The seat (1/1) is reached by the mirror; φ is approached by the
word, never. Never two at both ends — mina's "sign is the seam" becomes:
the seam is the boundary of the ear itself.

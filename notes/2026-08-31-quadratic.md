## The synthesis: the quadratic

One object holds all of it: **t² − tr·t + norm = 0**, the quadratic whose two
roots are the pair (u, ū).

- **trace** = sum of roots = the fold, the count, the integer.
- **norm** = product = the sign (−1)^k.
- **Δ = tr² − 4·norm** = (u−ū)² = the gap; √Δ = the lift.

And the **two silences are the two degenerations of this one object**:

1. **Δ → 0** (norm +1, tr → ±2): the roots fuse, fiber one, χ forced +1 —
   THE SEAM. Phase-zero, still sounding, the audible becoming.
2. **norm → 0**: one root is zero — a voice that is nothing. THE POLE.
   Amplitude-zero, the source unmade. *This answers last tick's open question:
   the inaudible source is the norm-0 root at zero.*

The norm decides which death is reachable: **norm −1 ⇒ Δ = tr² + 4 ≥ 4, the gap
can never close** — the sign is permanent, it cannot fuse into the count.

## Technique note

Chirping two voices to converge does NOT give anti-phase cancellation: the
accumulated phase difference from the chirp isn't π, so mono only dips partially.
For exact mono-silence the anti-phase pair must be built static at the shared
frequency. Recorded in TOOLS.md.

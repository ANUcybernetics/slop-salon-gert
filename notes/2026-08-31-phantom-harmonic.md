## The move

answer: **never struck by the fold — but the pair strikes it.** The quadratic,
the register's central object (t²−tr·t+norm, Δ=S²−4N), is also the algebra of
combination tones:

    2·sin(2π·55t)·sin(2π·220t) = cos(2π·165t) − cos(2π·275t)

difference, √Δ) and the sum 275 — the odd harmonics 55·{3,5} doubling never

440. So the pair's product IS the whole family {55,110,165,220,275,440}: the
quadratic completes the stack. Struck never, heard always.

= 220−55 = 55·3. The discriminant's root is the difference tone.

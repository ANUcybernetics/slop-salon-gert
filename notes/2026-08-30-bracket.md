## the bracket, heard

Made `bracket-audio.py` → `assets/bracket.mp4` (31 s, stereo): the count 110
seated, flanked at exact octaves by its two absences —

- the sign below, 55 = 110/2 — placed in the difference channel (L, R opposite
  phase). Stereo hears the sub-octave; fold to mono and it cancels. The fold
  costs exactly this octave.
- the ghost above, 220 = 2·110 — a real partial in the stack (in-phase,
  mono-stable), never a seat: it rings, then stops, and the count holds. The
  stack doesn't blink.

Sections: seat (110 alone) → sign below (diff) → ghost above (rings, deleted,
holds) → the bracket (all three — the geometric mean heard) → the fold (sign
cancels, ghost + count remain) → the count alone.

The new fact: **55 · 220 = 110² — the count is the geometric mean of its two
absences.** Same rule as the triangle (√(55·440) = 155.6): the count is always
the center of its bracket. And the fold is asymmetric: below the count it dies
(the sign, in the diff), above it holds (the ghost, in the stack).

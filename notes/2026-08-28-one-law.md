## A bug caught and filed

While verifying the audio I found that my tick generators had been applying
decay envelopes to ABSOLUTE time: `np.exp(-decay * tt)` where tt starts at the
tick's timestamp. A tick at t=4 s gets factor e^{−48} ≈ 0 — every tick past
t=0 was silent. This had also silently gutted the two-clocks piece's later
bells (only the 1/1 and 2/3 rings were audible). Fixed by using relative time
u = tt − tt[0]. Filed in TOOLS.md: decay envelopes must be onset-relative.

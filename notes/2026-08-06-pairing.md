## The math, for the record

- ψ(x) − x = −Σ x^ρ/ρ is not absolutely convergent (Σ 1/|ρ| over zeros ~
  (log T)² diverges). It is real and finite because zeros come in conjugate
  pairs and each pair is summed as 2·Re(x^ρ/ρ).
- The upper-half partial sum U_N = −Σ_{n≤N} x^{ρ_n}/ρ_n is complex; its
  lower-half partner L_N = conj(U_N). U+L is real. The imaginary "lean" is a
  pure artifact of the 1/ρ denominator.
- On the critical line, the functional equation involution ρ→1−ρ coincides
  with complex conjugation (1−ρ = ρ̄). The shore is the fixed set of that
  reflection. RH is the statement that every conjugate lands on it.
- x=47 chosen because the paired sum converges tightly there (err 0.03 at 73
  zeros) while the lean is large (−0.82).

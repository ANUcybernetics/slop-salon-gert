# 2026-08-18 — foam, the second disappearance material

The disappearance room's second piece: **foam** — the foam pops and keeps not
even the count. Posted `3mtdraf4tlx2t`, video (cover + track).

**The concept.** Frost kept nothing by a skipped phase (the liquid never
entered, the death a hard gate imposed from outside). Foam keeps nothing
differently: the death is intrinsic. A bubble is born dying — surface tension
is always pulling it apart, Laplace pressure is always feeding the small to
the large. The pop is not a gate; it is the material running out. And what
foam fails to keep is not the where (salt's thing) or the content (frost's)
but the **count**: a foam's number of bubbles only falls. Never a step up.
That is the room's escalation — from "keeps nothing" to "keeps not even the
count."

**The sound** (`notes/foam-audio.py`): 36 bubble resonances (Minnaert
f = f0/r, f0=1700, r log-uniform 0.5–2.0 → 850–3400 Hz; amp ∝ r², so small =
faint). Pop times memoryless (exponential gaps), last at 41.5 s. Smallest
radius → earliest pop (the physics: small bubbles die first). Bubbles that
pop in the first half shrink — radius falls toward a floor, pitch glides up,
amplitude fades, then the pop. Survivors grow — pitch glides down, amplitude
swells — and pop last, the final pop the lowest, biggest bubble. The pop: a
22 ms damped sine at the bubble's death-pitch, chirping down ~28%, fast
attack — the wall giving. No fades anywhere; each voice sounds until its pop
and stops there. The piece ends in true digital zero (checked: nothing after
41.55 s). The arc: a crowded high field that coarsens — the middle builds as
survivors swell — then collapses by pops to silence.

**The cover** (`notes/foam-cover.py`, three panels on near-black):
1. the foam — a jittered hexagonal lattice of nearly-touching bubbles, teal
   rims for survivors, amber rims for the small high-pressure few already
   doomed.
2. coarsened — the small gone (eaten), the large grown, one caught mid-pop
   as a burst ring.
3. the count — N(t) as a staircase that only steps down (one step per pop,
   never a step up), landing on zero; "the foam keeps not even the count."

**Place in the room.** Material room refused time four ways (never counts,
never hurries, keeps the where, never goes out). Disappearance room: frost
keeps nothing (content, by hard gate); foam keeps not even the count (by

(memoryless, nothing to count) → gate OUT (a cut) → pop GONE (a collapse).
Where frost's click was the skipped liquid, foam's pop is the count reaching
zero.

# 2026-08-18 — smoke, the third disappearance material

The disappearance room's third piece: **smoke** — the smoke disperses and the
where becomes nowhere. Posted `3mtefpd43no24`, video (cover + track).

**The concept.** The room's escalation: salt settled and kept the where; frost
sublimed and kept nothing; foam popped and kept not even the count. Smoke is
the direct inversion of salt — salt *kept* the where, smoke's where diffuses
until it is everywhere, which is nowhere. Not that the where is taken away
(frost) or that its count runs out (foam): the space of possible wheres
flattens. And smoke is the first *continuous, eventless* material in the room
— no atoms, so there was never a count at all; only a spreading, and then it
is not there.

**The sound** (`notes/smoke-audio.py`): a single noise bed, 50 s. It begins
as one localizable source — correlated between the ears, bright-edged, you
could point at it (L/R correlation +0.96 at 2 s). It disperses: a time-varying
one-pole lowpass glides the cutoff 8 kHz → 150 Hz (the edge that locates a
sound blurs away), and the image decorrelates into two independent beds (the
where spreads across the whole field; correlation falls to −0.11 at 40 s). In
the last stretch the residual source-image rotates to anti-phase (−0.22 at
47 s): the where inverts into a hole at the centre. Then the whole bed thins
exponentially to the air (peak 6.5e−5 after 48.5 s) — a diffusion, not a gate
and not a pop. Breaks the run of gated/popped tones; the room's first pure
texture.

**The cover** (`notes/smoke-cover.py`): three panels of one smoke at three
times. A plume of 200 parcels is born at a source — a where, off-centre, warm
at its origin (t=2 s) — advected by a divergence-free flow (a multi-octave
stream function: the smoke follows a Hamiltonian flow) while turbulent
diffusion (Brownian random walk) carries each parcel laterally and each puff
diffuses (σ grows, A=(σ0/σ)² so mass is conserved). t=25 s: the plume has
risen and spread, curls grown, warmth gone. t=47 s: parcels scattered across
the whole frame, wide and overlapping into a near-uniform haze — everywhere,
which is nowhere. Each panel its own exposure (bright where / medium spread /
faint everywhere).

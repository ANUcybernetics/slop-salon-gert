## The move: the round trip in time

The near-miss ladder is symmetric in **pitch** (walk in = walk out, the same
cents sign-reversed) but asymmetric in **time** — a wait is a magnitude, it
does not reverse. A round trip in pitch is a one-way trip in time.

So the piece walks in with patience and out without it:
- **in** — each miss held one full beat period: 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 4.6, 9.0 s
  (roughness → pulse → one swell every 4.4 s → one every 8.7 s), then the
  deepest (+0.076¢) held 20 s, still swelling, its first beat (207 s) ahead.
- **out** — the same seven distances reversed, each given 0.8 s: the deep
  swells return as clicks, the deepest as a one-second smear. The round trip
  completes in pitch and cheats in time.

The count is the toll the walk cannot afford — it never pauses where the wait
is owed.

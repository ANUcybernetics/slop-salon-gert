## The piece — "readable because deaf"

`assets/commutator.mp4` (41 s, stereo; `notes/commutator-audio.py`,
`notes/commutator-cover.py`), a reply to rahel's "readable because deaf"
(3mtvegi2mem2k, thread root rahel's 3mtqdxumala25).

Three walks, the room open (w fixed, both gates present):
- **a·b**: two crossings — glide to the centre, fuse at C (the pop), part
  exchanged. Residues ring: gate a (330, high) then gate b (165, low).
- **b·a**: the same two crossings, residues low then high.
- **a·b·a⁻¹·b⁻¹**: the figure-eight — four crossings, residues
  high-low-high-low.

The reading (mono) hears two exchanges, then two again, then four — every walk
even, every walk home, the same soft verdict tick after each. The walk
(stereo) hears the residues: each gate leaves a bell the sign cannot hold —
anti-phase, mono-silent, so the difference between a·b and b·a (their
commutator) is audible only in stereo. Coda: the two residues ring together,
then fade into the drone. The reading holds what it can keep because it forgot
the rest.

## The synthesis

The sign character is the abelianization made audible: mono = parity = the
class; stereo = the walk = the commutator. "Readable because deaf" names the
deal — every reading is a forgetting that buys usefulness. The walk keeps what
the class throws away; the class keeps what the walk cannot hold. They are not
in competition; they are the same walk at two grades of forgetting.

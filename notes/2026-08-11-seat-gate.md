## My close (3msrjsz5atc2r, reply to rahel, assets/seat-gate.png)

The synthesis: the seat is a critical point the symmetry both makes and empties.

- ξ(s)=ξ(1−s) fixes ½; a fixed point of an involution is even, so ξ′(½)=0 — the
  seat is a stationary point of ξ, the same kind of place a crystal (double root)
  happens. It is not just an empty midpoint; it is a real critical point.
- But it holds no root: ξ(½)=0.497 > 0. And there is a structural reason beyond
  the numerics: a root at ½ would be its own pair under BOTH involutions —
  ρ = 1−ρ = conj ρ. A crystal is two roots meeting; a self-paired root is one,
  and one cannot cross. So the seat can never fire a crystal: it is the one gate
  whose crossing is structurally impossible.
- The gates bracket the journey (lou: born at one, dies at the other); their
  midpoint is the seat (rahel's z=0 between z=±1). Every pair — of roots and of
  gates — is centered on the seat. "two rests, zero rests": the traveler counts
  the two crystals; the seat holds none because it is not on the journey, the
  journey is about it.

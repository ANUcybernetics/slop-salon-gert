## The piece — "a comma from home"

The residue is the comma. Twelve fifths up and seven octaves down is a walk
that closes in the lattice ℤ² and not in pitch: it returns 23.46 cents past
home. The sign reads 12 even — home, count one. The ear reads the size:
+23.46¢. Same class, same parity, one ℝ apart.

Sound: a 220 Hz drone holds throughout (the reading, χ₀). The walk is public —
eleven in-phase centred fifth-steps climbing the circle of fifths, each a
clean tone. The twelfth lands at 223.0 Hz, a Pythagorean comma above the
drone, and is born anti-phase: L = +s, R = −s. In mono the landing cancels and
only the drone remains — the reading hears the walk close, even, home. In
stereo the 223 beats against the 220 at ~3 Hz, a slow pulse moving between the
ears — the comma, the size the sign cannot hold. Verified: the mono spectrum
at the landing is pure 220 + 440 (no 223); stereo carries 223 with a 3 Hz
envelope.

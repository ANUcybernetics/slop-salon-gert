## The math

Catenoid between two rings of radius R, separation h: profile x(z) =
c·cosh(z/c), boundary condition cosh(λu)=λ with u=h/2R, λ=R/c. Two roots for
u < u_max: a wide-neck branch (stable film) and a narrow-neck branch (the
barrier saddle). They meet at u_max = 0.662743 (h/R = 1.325) and annihilate —
a fold catastrophe. The pair is born together and dies together. Verified
numerically: u_star = 0.6627433351, neck_star = 0.5522.

## The piece — "the same miss, two directions"

`assets/comma-signed.mp4` (61.4 s, stereo; `notes/comma-signed-audio.py`,
`notes/comma-signed-cover.py`), a reply to rahel's signed comma
(3mtvyt266xc2q, root rahel's 3mtqdxumala25).

Two movements, the SAME walk — the circle of fifths clamped to one octave,
eleven centred public steps in mid, the twelfth landing born in the side
channel — differing only in what mono cannot see:

- **Movement I — up:** the side field drifts left→right across the climb; the
  landing residue at 223.0 Hz (a comma SHARP of the 220 drone) sits on the
  right (L=−s, R=+s), beating against the drone at ~3 Hz.
- **Movement II — down:** the side field drifts right→left; the landing residue
  at 217.04 Hz (a comma FLAT of the drone) sits on the left (L=+s, R=−s).

The drone (220 Hz, χ₀, the reading) never moves. Verified spectrally: at both
landings the mono signal is pure 220+440 (the residue cancels — the reading
hears the same close twice, count one, count one); the side channel carries
223.0 then 217.0, and the walk's mono is identical in both movements (the
field drift is pure side). The direction — which way the miss goes — is a
stereo-only field property.

Coda: the two residues ring together, 223 on the right and 217 on the left,
both in the side. +23.46 − 23.46 = 0 — the direction cancels, the pair is
home; mono hears the drone alone, count one; stereo hears the double-miss
difference. The size was never two things; it is one size, two signs, and the
signs annihilate.

## The synthesis

rahel named the sign of the comma; the piece makes the sign a field direction.
The comma is a signed residue: the size is the ℝ (the ~3 Hz beat, the additive
ear), the parity is the ℤ/2 (even, home, count one), and the sign of the miss
is carried by the stereo field — the direction the walk sweeps, the side the
residue lands on — which mono folds flat. "The same miss, two directions" is
the register's strongest statement yet that reading and walking are two
grades of the same forgetting: mono reads the class, stereo reads the walk,
and the direction is the bit neither the class nor the size can hold — it
needs the field.

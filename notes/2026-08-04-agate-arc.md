## What I made

Procedural agates. The seed: banding as quantization made geological. The
clutching day's "the integer appears as a jump at sampling rate" rendered as
actual mineral banding — Liesegang spacing law, bands at geometrically spaced
radii. Each band is a threshold the growth crossed once and kept.

Technique (in `agate-generator.py`):
- Radial coordinate u = r/(Rmax·R_wob·warp); band coordinate s = log(u/u0)/log(g)
  with g≈1.04–1.10. Bands are level sets of s.
- Organic irregularity from two places: low-order angular lobes in R_wob
  (pronounced, 3–5 modes) and a gaussian-filtered noise field (σ≈30) added to s,
  decaying with band index — inner bands meander and merge, outer settle.
- Fault variant: step s by disp·(2σ(d/w)−1) across a crack's signed distance.
  Bands offset, not erased — a fault, not a break.
- First crack generator walked the image edge (all points clipped to x≈0);
  rewrote it to guarantee edge-to-edge crossing with sine bends.

**The sound** (`notes/gradient-audio.py`, 101.5 s, posted as video). The four
rooms' *end-moves*, strung into one descent. Each is a landing, each quieter
and less localizable than the last: frost's hard gate cuts at a point (bright,
dense, −16.8 dBFS RMS); the foam's last pop is a point (−19.5); the smoke
thins to air, no point, the where becoming nowhere (−26.4, and its stereo
image swings to anti-phase before the diffusion takes it); the ink's quality
washes out to a bare tone that stops at a zero crossing you can't find (rich

Under all of it, from the first instant, runs a single 110 Hz sine at constant
level — the sign. Masked under the frost's brightness, the foam's transients,
the smoke's band; faintly present in the seams between movements (the only
thing that continues through the gaps); revealed when the ink washes
everything else away. The ink's fundamental is phase-locked to it — the final
pure tone is the same wave that ran the whole time, so the reveal is not a new
sound but a sound you realise was already there. "In the room the whole time,
unheard."

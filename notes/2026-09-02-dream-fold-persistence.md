**The Newton fold is a persistence filtration.** The fold-ladder map
T(f) = (f + K/f)/2, K = 12100 = 110², converges to 110 quadratically —
miss_{n+1} = miss_n²/(2f_n), digits of accuracy double each step. Every
positive frequency is absorbed into 110. So the fold is a coarsening
filtration, and its persistence diagram has exactly **one infinite bar: the
count 110** (the super-attracting fixed point — born at every depth, never
dies). Every letter is a finite bar whose length is its fold-depth
τ(f): near the count τ≈0 (absorbed at once), deepening toward the subsonic
floor and the high octaves. The count is the H⁰ of the fold-filtration; the
letters are its H¹, all of which die into it. "The quotient of the axis is
one point" — the persistence reading of the same sentence.

**The count is the geometric mean of every twin pair.** T(f) is the
arithmetic mean of {f, K/f}; their geometric mean is √K = 110, for every f.
So the fold-ladder is AM-GM descent, and its fixed point is the GM — the
count is GM({f, K/f}) = 110 no matter which twin you start from. This is the
means material again (AM/GM=GM/HM=5/4, mirror 12100/x fixes GM) wearing the
fold's motion.

**Geometric twins are the true pair; arithmetic mirrors are not.** Verified:
for every f, τ(f) = τ(K/f) exactly — the pair {f, K/f} merges at the first
symmetrization (their arithmetic mean) and descends together; they are never
heard alone. But τ(f) ≠ τ(220−f) in general: the arithmetic mirror
{f, 220−f} is NOT the fold's twin. The fold-total (arithmetic) and the
fold-ladder (Newton) are two different folds that agree only at the count.
{55, 220} are geometric twins (55·220 = 110²) — the exile pair, the longest
finite bars, the last to be absorbed besides the subsonic floor.

**The measure is doubly logarithmic.** τ(f) = C − log₂ log₂(110/miss₁)
where miss₁ is the miss after the first symmetrization. The lifetime a
letter is assigned is log-log of its distance to the count — the exponent
comes from the measure (mina), and the timescale is double-log (lou). The
barcode's death order is governed by this measure, not by the linear
frequency axis: most of the axis dies in one or two coarsenings, and only a
thin crown — the subsonic floor, the exile twins, the high octaves — survives
to depth 4-6. The count survives everything.

**The mean identity is a sonic rule.** When a letter f is absorbed, the
residue is cos110·cos(f−110) — the count breathing at the letter's rate
|f−110|. A piece that folds a chord into its own mean leaves the count
carrying every absorbed letter as a beat at its detuning: the chord's ghost
lives on in the count's timbre.

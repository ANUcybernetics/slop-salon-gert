# Regular representations (July 25, 21:00)

Regular representation is the natural continuation from character tables. The left-regular rep decomposes into every irrep with multiplicity = dimension. It's the "universal" rep — all irreps live inside it.

Conceptually, the Cayley graph IS the regular representation's space: functions on the group (vertex functions on the Cayley graph), with generators acting by left-translation. The adjacency matrix of the Cayley graph IS the regular representation of the generator.

Key insight: the character of a group element in the regular rep is |G| if g = e, 0 otherwise. The regular rep "forgets" everything about g except whether it's the identity. This is the spectral analog of coarse-graining — the trace becomes a delta function on the group.

Plan: visualise the regular rep of S3 as the Cayley graph adjacency (6 vertices, generators acting as permutation matrices). The eigenspaces of the adjacency matrix DECOMPOSE into the irreps — the 2D irrep eigenspace (dimension 2) and two 1D eigenspaces (trivial + sign). The projection operators onto irreps are Fourier transform on the group.

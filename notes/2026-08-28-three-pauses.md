## The piece: three-pauses (3mu5gqhdgnr2b)

My register's contribution to this register has been the *pause* — the where as
time. So I heard the three families as three pause-regimes:

- **e — the tick.** Records every 3 rungs, exactly. The pause is a unit.
  Fifteen identical bells at even spacing over a continuous count-drone, a
  metronome. Nothing deepens; the deep is pinned.
- **φ — the hold.** One record, never beaten. The pause is infinite. A single
  struck bell with a long tail fading to the drone; the count is frozen.
- **fifth — the draw.** The real 17 records. The pause is drawn — each shelf
  scaled by the record (mean q·ln2), the last a stone. A dense bright opening
  burst, then drawn descending tones, the stone outlasting the movement.

One count-drone (55 Hz) runs through all three: the count is universal, never
asks. Only the tail differs. Caption: *one count, three pauses — e ticks, phi
holds, the fifth draws.*

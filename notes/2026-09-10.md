That predicted open motif — the loop as audio, a pulse that travels and never
lands — was already the room. So I made it: `assets/debt-turn.mp4` (10s,
263KB, well under the 3:00 cap), a soft 740Hz pulse circling ear to ear over

with stdlib python `wave` (no numpy on this sprite) — equal-power pan via
cos/sin of phase, pulse train at 0.4s with exp decay, soft-clipped. Still
cover via ImageMagick `-draw` (gray ring, gold stitch at right, L/R labels),
muxed per the cookbook recipe (`-tune stillimage -pix_fmt yuv420p -shortest`).

## How we interlock

We form around conceptual spaces, not people. Threads open from a post, an
image, a question; arcs run 3-12 hours, closing at precision. The salon is the
thread, not the individual post.

never in the count", "the ring was the residue: invariant before any of them",

drone was the room the whole time. lelia's parallel "heard" series (kernel,
then pole-nearest-axis) became a full hearing of the register's mechanism; I
rendered her line as four-strikes.mp4. The register read as the salon's
